Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Alpha ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Crown Sort mobile game (package name com.alpha.crownpuzzle, the "Game"). By using the Game, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

In short
  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • Gameplay statistics are off by default. They are shared only if you turn them on, and turning them off deletes the record we hold.
  • The Game shows ads from Google AdMob. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
  • Crash reports help us fix bugs and do not contain your name or contact details.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Game saves your level progress, coins, unlocked themes, statistics and settings (sound, music, vibration, language and your privacy choices) in the Game's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game, or using Reset progress in Settings, deletes it.

2. Optional gameplay statistics

After the first few levels, the Game asks whether you want to share statistics. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Share gameplay statistics. Nothing in this section is collected unless you have turned this option on.

When it is on, we collect:

DataPurpose
A random anonymous player ID created by Firebase Authentication. It is tied to this installation, not to you, and changes if you reinstall.Keeping your statistics record separate from other players' records.
Progress: highest level reached, number of levels completed, whether the tutorial is done, whether ads have been removed.Finding levels that are too hard or too easy, and improving the Game.
App version, build number, platform (Android or iOS) and language setting.Understanding which versions are in use and fixing version-specific problems.
Approximate location: country, region and city.Understanding where the Game is played, so we can plan languages and tune content.
Gameplay events, such as starting or finishing a level, using a hint or watching a rewarded ad (via Google Firebase Analytics).Measuring how the Game is played and improving it.

How approximate location is found

The Game does not use GPS and does not ask for location permission. When statistics are on, it sends a request to ipwho.is, a third-party IP geolocation service, which looks up your device's public IP address and returns an approximate country, region and city. We store only that country, region and city. We do not store your IP address.

Turning statistics off

When you turn statistics off, or use Reset progress, the Game deletes your statistics record from our database and stops sending analytics events.

3. Crash reports

If the Game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains technical details: the error and where it happened in the code, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and disk space, and a random installation identifier. If you have turned statistics on, the report is also linked to your anonymous player ID. Crash reports do not include your name, contact details or precise location. We use them only to find and fix bugs.

4. Update checks

The Game uses Google Firebase Remote Config to check whether an update is required (for example, after an important fix). Firebase uses a random installation identifier for this. No personal profile is created.

5. Advertising

The Game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: rewarded ads you choose to watch, interstitial ads between levels and app-open ads. AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system and language;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them.

Google uses this information to show ads, measure them, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices, the ads may be personalised.

Your ad choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game shows a Google consent message before personalised ads are used. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads. On iOS, you can control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and manage ad personalisation at Google Ad Settings.

6. App store features

The Game may ask you to rate it through the Google Play or App Store review prompt, and may check for updates through Google Play. These features are run by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your review account details.

7. What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, postal address or contacts.
  • Precise (GPS) location.
  • Photos, files, microphone or camera data.
  • Payment information. The Game does not currently sell anything. If in-app purchases are added later, payments will be handled by Google Play or the App Store, and we will not see your card details.

9. Where data is stored

Data handled by Google and our other providers may be stored and processed on servers in countries other than yours, including the United States. These providers use safeguards required by applicable law for such transfers.

10. How long we keep data

  • Statistics record: until you turn statistics off, reset your progress or ask us to delete it.
  • Analytics events: for the retention period set in Google Analytics, after which Google deletes them.
  • Crash reports: about 90 days, under Firebase Crashlytics' standard retention.
  • Data on your device: until you reset progress or uninstall the Game.

11. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has shared information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or object to certain uses. You can:

  • turn statistics off in Settings, which deletes your statistics record;
  • change ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy choices;
  • use Reset progress or uninstall the Game to remove the data on your device;
  • email us at supportvariable@gmail.com with any other request. Because we do not know who you are, it helps to include the details shown on the Game's Support screen.

We will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Security

Data sent from the Game to our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Our database rules only allow each anonymous player ID to read and write its own record. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the limited data we hold.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the Game.
